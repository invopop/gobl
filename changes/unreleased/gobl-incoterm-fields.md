## Added

- `icc`: new International Chamber of Commerce catalogue with the `icc-incoterms-2010-rule` and `icc-incoterms-2020-rule` extensions for the Incoterms® 2010 and 2020 rule codes on delivery details.
- `untdid`: `untdid-delivery-terms` extension for the UNTDID 4053 delivery terms codes `1` (arranged by the supplier) and `2` (arranged by a logistic service provider) on delivery details.
