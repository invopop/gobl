## Added

- `bill`: `DeliveryDetails` gains `ext` for extension codes that apply to the
  delivery.
- `untdid`: `incoterm-delivery-terms` extension for the delivery terms, with
  the Incoterms and UNTDID 4053 codes the French CTC Extended rules accept
  (BR-FREXT-CL-27): `1`, `2`, `CFR`, `CIF`, `CIP`, `CPT`, `DAP`, `DDP`, `DPU`,
  `EXW`, `FAS`, `FCA` and `FOB`.
