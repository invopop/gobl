# v0.512.0 - 2026-11-11

## Added

- `addons`: approved `ro-efactura-v1` as an external addon implemented by [`github.com/invopop/gobl.ro.anaf`](https://github.com/invopop/gobl.ro.anaf), so it is now a valid `$addons` value.
- `ro`: new tax regime for Romania, with the VAT rate history, CIF/CUI tax identity validation, and credit note and corrective invoice corrections.
- `ro`: examples for the most common Romanian invoices: domestic, in EUR, to a consumer, with domestic reverse charge, and a credit note.
- `convert`: `Input.Requested` holds the format keys an import was limited to, so a detector can claim a requested format it would not pick on its own, such as one whose documents only differ from another's by their container.
- `cz`: new tax regime for the Czech Republic, with CZK as the currency, VAT at 21 % standard and 12 % reduced (with the rate history since 2010), DIČ validation for legal and natural persons, and an example domestic invoice.
- `icc`: new International Chamber of Commerce catalogue with the `icc-incoterms-2010` and `icc-incoterms-2020` extensions for the Incoterms® 2010 and 2020 rule codes on delivery details.
- `untdid`: `untdid-delivery-terms` extension for the UNTDID 4053 delivery terms codes `1` (arranged by the supplier) and `2` (arranged by a logistic service provider) on delivery details.

## Changed

- `convert`: **breaking**: `Converter.Import` takes the `*ImportOptions`, as `Export` takes the `*ExportOptions`, so converters receive the requested formats.
